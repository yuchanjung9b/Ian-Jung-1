What is this website?

Korea Travel Guide collects the useful basics for a trip to South Korea in one place. Pick a province and you will find famous sights, hotels for every budget, local dishes to try and good restaurants, each with an approximate price in Korean won and US dollars. There is no sign-up and nothing to book here. It is a starting point for your own plan.

About Korea 대한민국

South Korea sits on the Korean Peninsula in East Asia. Its capital, Seoul, is one of the world's great cities, and the country packs a lot into a small area: royal palaces and mountain temples, hiking trails and beaches, volcanic Jeju Island, and modern cities full of neon, cafés and K-pop. Korean food, from barbecue to street snacks, is a reason to visit all by itself.

  • CapitalSeoul (서울)
  • LanguageKorean, written in Hangul (한글)
  • CurrencyKorean won (₩)
  • SeasonsFour distinct seasons. Spring blossoms and autumn leaves are the favorites.
  • Getting aroundFast trains (KTX), subways and buses connect nearly every city.
